Junior Software Engineer

We are hiring a junior engineer to join our backend team. This is a role for someone early in
their career who wants to learn quickly.

You will need:
- Some experience writing Java or Kotlin, whether commercial, academic or personal projects
- An understanding of how relational databases work and the ability to write SQL
- Willingness to learn and take feedback
- English good enough to read documentation and participate in code review

We do not expect you to know our whole stack. We use Spring Boot, Postgres and Git, and we will
teach you what you do not know.
